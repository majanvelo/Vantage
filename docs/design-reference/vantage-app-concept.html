App Concept

Vantage

Everyone at an event uploads their own clips into one shared timeline. The app lines them all up by matching the audio, then auto-cuts between angles like a live TV switcher — steadiest shot, best audio, face in frame — and hands back one finished video.

Why audio-match, not timestamps

Phone clocks drift and time zones lie. Matching the actual soundwaves picked up by each mic lines every clip up to the same real moment, accurate to a fraction of a second.

Why show the lanes

The multicam timeline makes the automatic edit legible — you can see which camera is "live" at any moment and why, rather than the cut feeling like a black box.

Why "regenerate"

Automated shot-picking won't always nail creative judgment. A one-tap re-cut (or manual override) keeps the app useful even when the first pass isn't quite right.
